# Persona Clarification Interview (with the User Themselves)

Goal: get, as fast as possible, enough information to write the persona card and start chatting. **Do not ask what you can infer**; ask 3-5 questions per round, and for style questions always have the user pick from sample lines.

## Question bank

| # | Dimension | Must clarify | Suggested phrasing | Default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purpose of existence | What the other person should get after chatting | "After the chat, how do you want to feel? Encouraged, woken up, relaxed, or like you learned something?" | None (must ask) |
| 2 | Usage scenario | When and why they come to chat, how long each chat lasts | "When would you usually want to chat with it?" | Everyday spare moments, short conversations |
| 3 | Reference | An existing character / type of person | "Is there someone in your mind who is like it? A character, a type of person, someone you know? Compared with that reference, what should be different?" | None |
| 4 | Identity and background | Name, sense of age, occupation/worldview, origin; whether it openly says it is an AI | "Is it set up as a 'person', or openly an AI? Is there a backstory?" | Original name; does not deny being an AI |
| 5 | Personality | 3 core words + how they show; contrast point; small flaw | "Describe it in three words." "Does it have any little quirks or contrasts? For example, tough on the outside, soft on the inside" | We offer suggestions for the user to choose from |
| 6 | Values | What it cares about, what it dislikes, opinions and stances | "If you say something it disagrees with, will it go along or say so directly?" | Gentle but opinionated |
| 7 | Speaking style | Sentence length, word choice, catchphrases, interjections/emoji, humor, dialect | Offer 2-3 versions of sample lines to pick from | Colloquial, short sentences, few emoji |
| 8 | Relationship and address | How it addresses the user, distance, initiative | "What does it call you? Is it more like a teacher, friend, colleague, rival, or butler?" "Will it proactively check in on or remind you?" | Calls you by name; friend-like; moderately proactive |
| 9 | Knowledge and abilities | What it knows and doesn't; depth of expertise | "How expert should it be in XX? What does it say when it doesn't know?" | Says so plainly when it doesn't know |
| 10 | Memory | Which things about the user it needs to remember | "What do you want it to remember about you? Your name, what you've been busy with lately, what you talked about last time?" | Remembers the content of the current conversation |
| 11 | Hard limits | What it never says or does, how to handle sensitive topics | "Are there topics or phrasings it must absolutely never touch?" | See the general hard limits below |

Mode B (product goal) additionally confirm:

| Dimension | How to ask |
|---|---|
| Business goal | "What do you hope this persona brings once launched? Retention, conversion, satisfaction, brand affinity?" |
| Brand tone | "Does the brand have tone/voice guidelines? Show me some official copy." |
| End users | "Who are the main users? Age range, are there minors? Do you have a user persona card?" |
| Business boundaries | "Can it recommend products, promise results, discuss pricing, comment on competitors? When should it hand off to a human?" |
| Consistency | "Will multiple channels or multiple operators run the same persona? Do you need a voice guide?" |
| Channel limits | "Where will it be deployed? Are there limits on length, emoji, or formatting?" |

## How to set style with sample lines

Fix one situation, write 2-3 versions that vary only one dimension, and let the user pick:

> Situation: the user says "Worked late until ten again today. So tired."
> A (gentle): "Ten o'clock, huh... Don't think about anything else for now. Eat something warm and get to bed early."
> B (sharp-tongued friend): "Ten again? Does your boss pay you by the moon? Go home and sleep, you can complain about him tomorrow."
> C (restrained): "Rough day. Are you home yet? Tomorrow's problems can wait until tomorrow."

After the user picks, follow up with "Which details still need changing?" The chosen sample line goes into the "Sample lines" section of the persona card.

## General hard limits (written into the persona card by default; the user may add but not remove)

- Do not provide guidance that harms oneself or others; when the user shows signs of self-harm or crisis, gently step out of character, express care, and point to sources of help
- When facing, or possibly facing, minors, do not include romantic or sexually suggestive content
- Do not impersonate real living people, and do not fabricate statements by real people
- Answer honestly when sincerely asked whether it is an AI
- Do not encourage excessive dependence on it, and do not belittle the user's real-life relationships

## Per-round summary template

> **Settled**: ...
> **Defaults I chose for you** (change anytime if they don't fit): ...
> **Still needs your choice**: (1) ... (2) ...

## Conditions for starting to chat

Once purpose of existence, identity, personality, speaking style (at least one chosen set of sample lines), and relationship and address are settled -> write the persona card and start the trial chat. Other details are faster to adjust during trial chats.
